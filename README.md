# Smart Care for Happy Pets 🐾

An all-in-one AI-powered care assistant for **any pet, any species**, with unlimited pets per account.

## Key Features

1. **Authentication & Multi-Pet Architecture**
   - Email + password, Google sign-in option, and instant Guest Mode.
   - Unlimited pets per account with free-text species input (not restricted to a dropdown).
   - Pre-loaded with 4 distinct species (Dog, Cockatiel, Betta Fish, Bearded Dragon).
   - Quick-switch pet carousel with live happiness score and streak badges.

2. **24/7 Species-Tailored AI Assistant & Vision Triaging**
   - Tailored advice per species category (mammal, bird, aquatic, reptile, amphibian, exotic).
   - Instant urgent emergency symptom detector (bleeding, seizures, breathing distress, toxicity) with 1-tap call to nearest emergency vet.
   - Photo-based Vision Symptom Checker for preliminary screening and veterinary guidance.

3. **Species-Adapted Health & Daily Care Hub**
   - Daily care checklist adapted to pet species with celebration confetti upon 100% completion.
   - Weight & growth tracker with visual trend charts.
   - Medication and supplement reminders with "Mark as Taken" tracking.
   - Expense tracker with categorized breakdown and running totals per pet.

4. **Multi-Store Shopping Assistant (Price & Delivery Comparison)**
   - Free-text product search for any pet product (e.g., "dog food", "aquarium filter", "reptile UV lamp").
   - Species-adapted quick-search chips based on active pet.
   - Side-by-side comparison across Amazon, Chewy, Petco, and Flipkart.
   - Badges: **"Lowest price"**, **"Fastest delivery"**, and highlighted green **"Arriving soon"** (tomorrow/2-days).
   - Clearly labeled **"Best overall pick"** with reasoning.
   - Authentic outbound retailer links opening directly in new tabs.

5. **GPS-Enabled Vet Clinic Locator**
   - Real GPS distance sorting with interactive Map & List views.
   - Tap-to-call direct phone dialer.
   - Real website links and open/closed hours.
   - Highlighted specialty tags matching active pet species (avian care, aquatic, reptile, 24/7 emergency).
