1. Executive Summary

GrowPet is an advanced, multi-species digital care management platform engineered for pet owners across all animal classifications: Mammals, Birds, Aquatic Fish, Reptiles, Amphibians, and Exotic Pets. The system provides automated habit care generators, AI-powered triage and symptom analysis, emergency GPS veterinary locator, multi-store price aggregation, and instant real-time multi-browser cloud synchronization.

2. Functional Scope & Delivered Modules

🐶🐱 Universal Pet Profiles

Unlimited pet profile creation with intelligent species auto-detection, avatar gallery (cats, dogs, birds, reptiles, fish), and direct device photo upload via Base64.

📅 Tailored Care & Streak Engine

Automated daily care checklists tailored to species biology. Live calculation of Happiness Score (0–100%), care streaks, and celebratory confetti animations.

🤖 AI Veterinary Care Consultant

Species-aware AI chat assistant providing immediate dietary analysis, symptom triage, emergency first-aid protocols, and persistent chat cloud memory.

📍 GPS Vet Locator & Emergency Routing

High-accuracy dual GPS and IP Geolocation fallback with 1-click popular city shortcut filters, phone dialing, and direct Google Maps turn-by-turn routing.

💊 Medication & Health Records Vault

Track medication schedules, dosage instructions, last-given timestamps, weight tracking history (grams and kg), and veterinary medical visits.

🛒 Multi-Store Price Comparison

Aggregated price comparison across major pet retailers (Amazon, Chewy, Petco, PetSmart, Walmart) with expense categorization and budget analytics.

☁️ 1-Click Passwordless Auth & Real-Time Cloud Sync

Frictionless passwordless email authentication with deterministic user UID generation. Full cross-browser and cross-device synchronization powered by Firebase Cloud Firestore live snapshot listeners.
